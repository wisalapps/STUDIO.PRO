About omani.apps

omani.apps is an independent development team from Oman building native, Arabic-first apps for Android and Windows. Our first app, STUDIO PRO, is a multitrack studio for nasheed and vocals with maqam pitch correction, vocal roles, human-voice rhythms and high-quality export. It works fully offline, has no ads, and collects no personal data.
